Rye kvass

The original

The classic Russian kvass, made our way.

Rye kvass is the original: rye, water, sugar and a living culture, fermented briefly so it stays light and gently sparkling. Ours is fermented with raw makgeolli and cane sugar, for a clean, crisp, balanced sour-sweet.

How it is made

Classic and ours
Classic rye kvassOurs
BaseRye bread or rye malt, and waterToasted rye bread, steeped and strained
CultureBaker’s yeast, or a starter that also carries lactic acid bacteriaRaw makgeolli: its yeast and lactic acid bacteria
SugarUsually sugar; some recipes use honey or molassesCane sugar
TasteSweet and sour, bready, lightly sparklingClear and crisp, with a soft, balanced lactic sourness
