About Enzo

Curiosity is the starting point.

A wine background. An open mind.
A world of things worth tasting.

Enzo is a New York-based mixologist, beverage researcher, and hospitality consultant. His wine knowledge developed during his university years in California; his curiosity extends to spirits, beer, coffee, tea, global spices, and culinary traditions.

Exchanges with bartenders at Hope & Sesame’s Shenzhen location helped shape his approach. Exploring drinking cultures in Ginza, Bali, Barcelona, and the Caribbean brought new perspectives on flavor, context, and the rituals around a drink.

Today, his practice moves between original drinks, fermentation, independent bar consulting, and creative collaborations. The question stays simple: what would make this guest happiest?

Hospitality

The right drink begins with listening.

Complexity in Understanding.
Simplicity in Execution.

Technique is valuable when it helps us understand ingredients, balance, dilution, and people. A spritz belongs to a drinking culture. A tea cocktail can find clarity through soda dilution. Context changes the decision.

A moment across the bar

A guest wanted sharp, non-fruity acidity and a light drink. Enzo chose a Manzanilla Tonic—a response to the guest’s description, with hospitality guiding the technique.

Fermentation lab

Time is an ingredient.

Complete beverages.
Curiosity, allowed to develop.

Influenced by Noma’s fermentation philosophy, Enzo develops fermented drinks that stand on their own. His research brings sensory judgment into conversation with practical beverage production.

Lab notes
SubjectNotes
TepacheTwo expressions. One starting point. A traditional piloncillo version and a lighter expression made with refined cane sugar. Both have been commercially served.
KvassGrain, season, possibility. Explorations include rye kvass and seasonal pumpkin kvass, developed as beverages in their own right.
From research to serviceUnderstanding the whole process. Production experience includes beer-industry canning methods, connecting beverage development with real-world service.
